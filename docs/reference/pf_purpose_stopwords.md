# Funding boilerplate

<div class="ref-description section level2">

Words that say a grant was *made* rather than what it was *for*.
Removing them is what distinguishes a purpose that carries information
from one that does not — and it is why a character-length gate does not
work: 55 purposes of 12 characters or fewer are contentful
(`SCHOLARSHIP`, `EDUCATION`, `Culture`), while 23 purposes over 30
characters are not (`TO SUPPORT THE MISSION OF THE ORGANIZATION`).

</div>

<div class="section level2">

## Usage

<div class="sourceCode">

``` r
pf_purpose_stopwords()
```

</div>

</div>
